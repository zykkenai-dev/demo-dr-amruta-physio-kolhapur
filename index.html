Conditions

Where does it hurt?

Pick an area. Each plan starts with an assessment, so the treatment matches the cause, not just the pain.

Back, neck & spondylosis

Desk posture, long rides and lifting all add up. Cervical and lumbar spondylosis, slipped-disc pain and stiffness are treated with manual therapy and a home routine you can keep.

  • Cervical & lumbar spondylosis
  • Sciatica and lower-back pain
  • Postural neck pain

Knees, hips & chronic joint pain

From a parent's long-standing knee pain to recovery after an injury: strength, mobility and confidence, built step by step.

  • Chronic knee pain & arthritis
  • Post-injury and post-operative rehab
  • Balance and walking training

Shoulder & upper limb

Frozen shoulder, rotator-cuff strain and wrist problems, with movement restored gradually and without guesswork.

  • Frozen shoulder
  • Tailbone (coccyx) and referred pain
  • Wrist and elbow strain

Nerve conditions, including Bell's palsy

Sudden facial weakness is frightening. A calm, accurate diagnosis and a clear explanation of what's happening make all the difference, followed by targeted rehab.

  • Bell's palsy (facial nerve)
  • Neurological rehab
  • Numbness and tingling assessment
